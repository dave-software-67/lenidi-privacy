Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lenidi ("we", "our", "us"). Lenidi is a marketplace app that connects buyers and sellers in Ghana. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

By using Lenidi, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: Your full name, email address, and phone number when you sign up.
  • Profile information: Your profile photo (if you upload one).
  • Listing information: Product titles, descriptions, prices, photos, category, condition, and details you provide when posting an ad.
  • WhatsApp number: If you provide one for buyers to contact you.
  • Communication: Messages you send through our in-app AI chat assistant.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information: Device type, operating system, and app version.
  • Usage data: How you interact with listings, chats, and features.
  • Location: The region you enter when posting an ad (we do not track your GPS location).

2.3 Payment information

All payments for boosts and subscriptions are processed by Paystack. We do not store your card details, mobile money numbers, or bank information on our servers. Paystack handles all payment data securely according to their own privacy policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your Lenidi account.
  • Display your listings to potential buyers.
  • Enable communication between buyers and sellers via WhatsApp.
  • Process payments for boosts and subscriptions.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, scams, and abuse.
  • Respond to your support requests through our AI assistant.
  • Send you important updates about the app (only if necessary).
  • Improve our services and features.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data only with:

  • Paystack — to process payments for boosts and subscriptions.
  • Supabase — our secure cloud database provider (data is encrypted in transit and at rest).
  • Groq — the AI service that powers our Lenidi AI chat assistant. Messages you send to the AI are processed by Groq.
  • Other Lenidi users — your name, listings, and contact information are visible to buyers when you post an ad.
  • Legal authorities — only when required by law or to prevent serious harm.

Your WhatsApp number is only visible to buyers who view your listings. We do not display your email or phone number publicly unless you choose to include it in your ad.

5. Data Retention

  • Free accounts: If you do not open the app for 30 consecutive days and have no active subscription, your account and all your data will be automatically deleted.
  • Paid accounts: Your data is retained while your subscription (Plus, Pro, Premium, or VIP) is active.
  • After deletion: Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (visible in your Profile page).
  • Correct your name, email, or phone number at any time from Profile.
  • Delete your account permanently via Profile → Delete My Account.
  • Withdraw consent for AI chat processing by not using the AI feature.
  • Contact us for any privacy concerns.

7. Children's Privacy

Lenidi is intended for users 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with data should contact us.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) on our Supabase database.
  • Secure authentication for API access.
  • Regular security monitoring.

However, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information. Use strong passwords and never share your account credentials.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Your continued use of Lenidi after changes means you accept the new policy.
